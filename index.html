About

I am a Speech Technology Researcher and Data Scientist with expertise in speech enhancement, speech recognition, and multi-speaker analysis. Currently working at ConnexAI, I specialize in developing cutting-edge solutions for automatic speech recognition (ASR) and text-to-speech (TTS) systems. My research focuses on deep learning approaches to speech separation in challenging acoustic environments, with applications ranging from telephony speech processing to voice cloning technologies.

I completed my PhD at the University of Sheffield's Centre for Doctoral Training in Speech and Language Technology, where my thesis explored speech separation in noisy and reverberant acoustic environments. My work has been published in top-tier conferences including ICASSP, EUSIPCO, and ASRU, and I have contributed to multiple CHiME workshops.
